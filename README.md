# My Favorite Movies

I have a few movies that I really love! Here are some of them:

---

## 1. **Suzume** (2022)
> *Directed by Makoto Shinkai*

- A beautiful mix of fantasy and emotion  
- The story follows **Suzume**, a girl who discovers mysterious doors across Japan  
- The animation and music are *absolutely stunning*  

**Favorite Quote:**  
> “It’s not the end. We’ll open the door again.”

---

## 2. **Weathering With You** (2019)
> *Also directed by Makoto Shinkai*

- A touching story about love, sacrifice, and the weather  
- The visuals are breathtaking and the soundtrack by *RADWIMPS* is amazing  

**Main Characters:**
1. Hodaka Morishima  
2. Hina Amano  

**Fun Fact:**  
This movie is connected to *Your Name* — look out for the cameo!

---

## 3. **Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse** (2023)
> *Directed by Joaquim Dos Santos, Kemp Powers, and Justin K. Thompson*

- A masterpiece of animation and storytelling  
- Features multiple versions of Spider-Man across the multiverse  
- Each art style represents a different universe  

**Best Part:**
- The dynamic between **Miles Morales** and **Gwen Stacy**  
- The soundtrack perfectly matches the action scenes  

---

### Final Thoughts

All these movies have amazing visuals, emotional stories, and memorable characters.  
I’d recommend **anyone** who loves *animation* and *deep storytelling* to watch them!
